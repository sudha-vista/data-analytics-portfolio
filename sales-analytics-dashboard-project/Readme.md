# Sales Analytics Dashboard 

## Project Overview

Built a **Sales Analytics Dashboard** to analyze sales data and uncover **revenue trends, customer behavior, and growth opportunities**.  
Utilized **Python and SQL** to convert raw data into actionable insights, enabling **data-driven business decisions**.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze monthly revenue performance  
- Identify top customers contributing to sales  
- Segment customers based on value  
- Provide insights to improve business growth  

---

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)** – Data analysis & visualization  
- **SQL (SQLite)** – Data querying & aggregation  
- **Excel** – Data preparation  
- **Google Colab** – Development environment  

---

## Key Metrics

- Total Revenue: **$9.76M**  
- Total Orders: **300**  
- Total Customers: **28**  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Revenue shows a **consistent upward trend**, indicating strong business growth  
- **October** recorded the highest revenue ($1.66M)  
- Significant growth acceleration observed between **June and August**  

---

## 👥 Customer Insights

- Top customers (Varun, Ritu, Arun) contribute a major share of revenue  
- High dependency on a few customers highlights **business risk**  
- Opportunity to improve **customer retention and lifetime value**  

---

## Customer Segmentation

- High Value Customers: **32%**  
- Low Value Customers: **43%**  
- Medium Value Customers: **25%**  

- Majority customers fall under **low-value segment**, indicating potential for:
  - Upselling  
  - Cross-selling  
  - Targeted campaigns  

---

## Business Impact

- Identified **revenue growth patterns** to support strategic planning  
- Highlighted **customer concentration risk**  
- Provided insights to **increase customer lifetime value and revenue optimization**
